# Contributing and corrections

Corrections from vendors, users, researchers, and journalists are welcome.

## Submit a correction

Open a GitHub issue with:

1. the service name;
2. the field that should change;
3. the replacement value;
4. a direct URL to an official product, pricing, help, or terms page;
5. the date you verified the page;
6. a short explanation of the discrepancy.

Screenshots may be attached for context, but a reproducible public URL is preferred. Affiliate links and third-party reviews are not accepted as primary evidence.

## Review policy

- Evidence is checked against the field definitions in `METHODOLOGY.md`.
- Unknown values are not converted to “Yes” or “No” without explicit official evidence.
- Official contradictions are preserved in `conflict_notes`.
- Material changes are released as a new dataset version.
- No payment is accepted for inclusion, ranking, correction, or removal.

## Add a service

A proposed service should provide a hosted digital business card or a closely related vCard/QR product, have a public official website, and document enough of the evaluated fields to make the row useful.

## Responsible disclosure

Do not publish passwords, private account data, personal contact records, unpublished security issues, or private correspondence in an issue. Send sensitive security reports directly to the affected vendor.
